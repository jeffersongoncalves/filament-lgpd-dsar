## 2.0.0 - 2026-11-16

Filament panel for [laravel-lgpd-dsar](https://github.com/jeffersongoncalves/laravel-lgpd-dsar): data subject requests (LGPD art. 18).

- Register a request for any of the 9 rights, with the legal deadline
- Verify identity and link the data subject before anything is exported or erased
- Fulfil access/portability (downloadable JSON export) and anonymization/deletion
- Complete manual rights with the response given, or reject with a reason
- Overdue requests flagged, overdue filter, navigation badge with open requests
- English and Brazilian Portuguese (Filament 4)
